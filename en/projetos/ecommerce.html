03 / COMMERCE · WEB

E-commerce SaaS

Creating and managing online stores on a multi-tenant platform.

  • JavaScript
  • Firebase
  • Chart.js

Conceptual artwork · product screenshots coming soon

01Context

An online store platform needs to bring together the shopping experience and the administrative workflows of different stores.

02Goal

Enable users to create and manage stores with storefronts, orders, products, inventory and reports.

03Solution

A multi-tenant SaaS platform with customizable storefronts, a persistent shopping cart and an ordering flow, supported by administrative tools.

Key features

  • Customizable storefront, persistent shopping cart and ordering flow.
  • 4 administrative modules, including product CRUD, orders and inventory.
  • 4 KPIs and 2 charts.
  • Filterable reports with CSV export.

04Architecture and technologies

JavaScript and Chart.js in the application, with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Storage.

05Technical considerations

The multi-tenant context requires careful separation of store data. The specific isolation strategy has not been provided and remains to be documented.

06Experience and accessibility

The solution combines features for the shopping journey and store administration. Details of usability and accessibility validation have not yet been provided.

07Project status

The code and instructions for running and testing the MVP are available on GitHub. Payment and shipping are simulated; see the README for current limitations.
