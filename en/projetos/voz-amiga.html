02 / ACCESSIBILITY · MOBILE

Voz Amiga

Augmentative and Alternative Communication with pictograms and speech.

  • Ionic
  • Angular
  • TypeScript
  • Firebase

Conceptual artwork · product screenshots coming soon

01Context

Augmentative and Alternative Communication provides tools for people who need other ways to express their needs and communicate.

02Goal

Provide access to pictographic phrases and Portuguese text-to-speech in a mobile app.

03Solution

The app organizes 36 pictographic phrases into 6 categories, combining speech playback, saved favorites and accessibility preferences.

Key features

  • 36 pictographic phrases across 6 categories.
  • Brazilian Portuguese text-to-speech (pt-BR).
  • 4 authentication flows and route protection.
  • 3 accessibility preferences and saved favorites.

04Architecture and technologies

Ionic and Angular with TypeScript and SCSS for the interface; Capacitor for the mobile app and Firebase for service integration.

05Technical considerations

Coordinating text-to-speech, preferences and saved favorites is an important technical consideration for a consistent experience.

06Experience and accessibility

Categories, pictograms and favorites help organize access to phrases. Three accessibility preferences are documented; their details need confirmation before a more specific description can be provided.

07Project status

The project documents the features presented here and has a public repository. User testing results and adoption data have not been provided.
