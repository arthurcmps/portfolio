01 / FITNESS · WEB & MOBILE

Okan

A platform connecting trainees, personal trainers and gyms.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Firebase

Conceptual artwork · product screenshots coming soon

01Context

Workout tracking involves trainees, professionals and gym management, with different needs at each stage of the experience.

02Goal

Bring workout planning and operations management together in a mobile and web fitness platform.

03Solution

The app serves trainees and personal trainers, while the web interface provides administrative features for manager and superadmin roles.

Key features

  • Weekly planning and timed workouts.
  • History, charts, chat and push notifications through FCM.
  • Gym location registration, trainer management and licenses.
  • Subscriptions through Mercado Pago and workout plan templates.

04Architecture and technologies

Flutter and Dart in the app, integrated with Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions and App Check. The web application uses role-based access control (RBAC).

05Technical considerations

The combination of roles and interfaces makes access control and integration between workout and management data central to the architecture.

06Experience and accessibility

Experiences are organized around each person's role: training and tracking on mobile, administration on the web.

07Project status

The documented scope covers the mobile and web modules described in this case study. Repositories are linked below; app store availability has not been confirmed for this version.
